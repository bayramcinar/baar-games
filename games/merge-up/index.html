2048: Merge UP

Shoot numbered balls toward the ceiling and merge matching numbers. Aim for 2048, clear level goals, or see how long you can keep the board alive in Endless Mode.

Google Play App Store

How to play

  1. Aim your launcher

    Drag to line up a shot. Bounce a ball off either wall to reach a match tucked behind the stack.

  2. Match equal numbers

    Two balls with the same number combine into one with double the value. Set up nearby matches to chain merges together.

  3. Watch the danger line

    Balls collect at the top. Keep the stack from reaching the danger line as you build larger numbers.

What to explore

Levels
Reach a target number or score and earn stars as the goals get tougher.
Endless Mode
Play for a personal best and keep your stack clear for as long as you can.
Power-ups
Use the hammer to clear a ball, shuffle the stack, or swap the loaded ball for the next one.
