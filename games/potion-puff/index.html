Potion Puff: Color Sort

Turn a shelf of mixed potions into neatly sorted bottles. Plan each pour in a moonlit alchemy lab, or see how many puzzles you can solve against the clock.

How to play

  1. Pick a bottle

    Choose a bottle and check the color at the top. That layer is the one you can pour.

  2. Find a matching spot

    Pour into an empty bottle or one topped with the same color, as long as there’s space for the liquid.

  3. Sort the shelf

    Keep a spare bottle available to rearrange colors. Finish with a single color in each filled bottle.

What to explore

Play at your own pace
Solve bottle puzzles one pour at a time and plan moves before committing.
Timed challenge
Switch to a timed challenge when you want faster decisions and a different rhythm.
Alchemy workshop
Sort potions among glowing bottles, wooden shelves, and moonlit windows.
