Arrow logic puzzle

Smashkin

Every arrow has a direction and every shot changes the board. Work out the right order, use mirrors to redirect shots, and clear crates and jars in a colorful workshop.

App Store

How to play

  1. Read the board

    Check where each arrow points and which crates, jars, or obstacles stand in its path.

  2. Plan the shot order

    Break a crate to open a route for the next arrow. Use mirrors to reach targets around corners.

  3. Clear the targets

    Work through frozen glass and reinforced crates as the grids get tougher. Use each shot where it makes the biggest difference.

What to explore

120 handcrafted levels
Learn the basics, then solve trickier boards and earn up to three stars per level.
Endless Mode
Take on fresh boards, build a streak, and aim for a new high score.
Helpful boosters
Use hints and boosters when you need help getting past a tricky arrangement.
